Uttar Pradesh Vidhan Sabha Election

Face-authenticated voting demo for the UP state assembly election. Verify once with your face, vote for your constituency's MLA candidate — with your choice recorded in complete secrecy.

Register to vote → I'm already registered

How it works

  1. Register once — select your constituency, capture your face. Your face encoding is encrypted and stored, never the raw photo.
  2. Verify at polling time — enter your Voter ID and look at the camera. A live blink check confirms you're a real person, not a photo.
  3. Vote for your MLA — you see only candidates from your constituency. Your ballot is cast with no link back to your identity.

Demo notice: This is a proof-of-concept system for educational purposes. Fictional parties and candidates are used. Not for use in any real or binding election.
